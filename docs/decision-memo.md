# Decision memo — adopt the research process; allocate0% live capital

**Committee ask:** adopt Trust Planner for research and periodic decision review; authorize **0–0% of long-term capital for live allocation**. This app is a useful conditional decision-support process, not evidence sufficient to deploy trust assets. The same decision applies after the locked inflation change.

## Mandate and reference

The fictional trust starts with **$10million** and four children aged8/6/4/2 on **November23,2026**. Pay **$50,000 per child annually for all remaining college semesters**, half on August1/January1 through graduation. Adult payments begin one month after graduation; the **$20,000 annual minimum** is protected while the **$200,000 goal** is flexible. Benefits index monthly from model start, including deflation. The scenario assumes **2% annual future inflation**, zero optional expenses, and ends after scheduled payments through **November23,2104**, when the youngest reaches80. Principal may decline. Risk and protected payments outrank the10% nominal return objective.

The amended Lab13 mandate uses seven long-only ETFs, VOO as S&P500 proxy, **≤25% historical annualized volatility** and **≤33% proposed one-way annual turnover**. This initial deployment is explicitly excluded from turnover; it is not an assertion that later trading is free of the limit. SGOV backs the full undiscounted CPI-adjusted college reserve as a classroom assumption.

## Evidence and interpretation

The October22,2026 frozen adjusted-price snapshot ends September30,2026. Its36-return estimation period begins October31,2023. The decision-date staleness is54days, within60. A **+15.77% trailing12-month VOO signal** admits the risk-on equity ceiling. The grid’s Balanced Growth candidate is **70% SGOV,20% QQQ,10% XLE**, with all other weights0. This passes the modelled hard checks. Historical annualized volatility is **3.50%**; the **9.94% annualized arithmetic estimate misses the10% target**, although historical monthly-rebalanced CAGR is **10.34%**. These are different measurements; the target is tested against arithmetic return, not CAGR.

Equal weight fails the Balanced Growth equity ceiling. Inverse volatility passes. VOO alone fails college backing, the case equity ceiling and its per-equity-fund cap. The candidate initially earmarks **$800,000** for college; its college/residual long-term historical CAGR assumptions are **4.5312%/10.8519%**.

All reference payment paths complete. Adaptive ending balances are **$22,207,221,402.02 nominal** and **$4,738,935,797.77 in model-start purchasing power**. These multi-billion-dollar outputs expose the consequences of compounding one short historical sample for78years; they are **not wealth forecasts**.

The combined loss/higher-inflation/lower-return/higher-cost case stops fixed-goal payments on **November30,2059**, but adaptive support completes with **$1,515,686.39** in ending purchasing power and no protected-payment shortfall. The12- and60-month window checks each move a largest fund weight by10percentage points; a60% whole-trust single-fund ceiling moves it by30points. A25bp opening cost increase and10% volatility ceiling retain the baseline mix. This supports studying the process, not treating one optimized mix as stable truth.

## Conditions and reversal triggers

Before any nonzero pilot, require independently checked reserve liquidity/liability matching, prospective evidence, realistic return uncertainty and an explicit pilot ceiling approved by committee. Stop/revise for any college-backing or hard-minimum shortfall, stale/quarantined data, exceeded risk/turnover budget, or material changes in costs/thesis. Future trading, taxes and rebalancing are not simulated. Do not waive hard obligations to rescue the return target.
